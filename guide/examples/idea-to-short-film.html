One idea, three shots, one short film

One sentence → a shot plan → three 5-second clips → one 15-second film.

Sampled frames · 2026-10-08 · Reported saved run: $0.39051. z-ai/glm-5.3-flash · bytedance-seedance-v1.5-pro

The Shot planner writes a HERO line, a STYLE line and three shots: setup, journey and payoff. Each Shot prompt writer copies the hero and style into a prompt for its own shot, so the three separate video calls draw the same subject. Seedance 1.5 Pro renders each shot, and Combine joins the clips in order. Inspired by <a href="https://x.com/aiaicreate/status/2107279553609830408">ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Studio</a> by @aiaicreate, which turns one idea into a planned multi-shot film.

Saved result

GLM 5.3 Flash plans three shots around one hero, a yellow paper boat, and writes a separate prompt for each shot. Seedance 1.5 Pro renders three silent 5-second 720p clips, and Combine joins them into one 15-second film. Shot 1: a hand sets the boat into a neon-lit gutter. Shot 2 restarts the launch with two hands, then the boat drifts down the gutter past pink and teal reflections. Shot 3: it floats from a stone channel out onto a sunrise harbor. The boat looks the same in every shot. The cuts are hard, and the film has no sound.

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Rewrite Film idea as one sentence with a clear hero and a beginning, middle and end. Change the planner's system prompt to get more or fewer words, or a different style. To change models, pick another video model on Shot 1, Shot 2 and Shot 3. To get sound, switch on Generate Audio in each shot's options (Seedance charges more for it), or add a Video edit sound pass after Combine. Running the graph makes three paid video calls plus four cheap text calls.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. The saved run reported $0.39: three Seedance 1.5 Pro clips at $0.13 each (720p, 5 s, audio off) and four GLM 5.3 Flash calls at about $0.0001 each. Combine runs in the browser for free. Prices and results vary.
  4. Open the Shot planner's output first: the HERO line is what keeps the boat the same across clips. Then watch the film for consistency, and check that each shot does its own job. In the saved run, shot 2 repeats the launch before it drifts down the gutter. Each clip is generated separately, so expect hard cuts.
