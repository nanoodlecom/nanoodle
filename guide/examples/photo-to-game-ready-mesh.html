References that went in

Upload one photo of one object and get back a textured triangle mesh of it. Gemini first surveys the photo — the object, the view, its materials and colours, the sides the photo hides, and the fragile details. Idol Max Turbo (Sperid Labs' mesh line, landed on NanoGPT the same day this example shipped) rebuilds the object as a 30,000-triangle GLB with UVs and the textures embedded — 2048px base-colour and normal maps plus metallic-roughness maps, PBR included in the $0.10. GLM then writes the guide: what you got, where it drops in, and the honest edges. In the saved run the golden dragon statue from Photo to 3D Splat — antiqued bronze with dark patina, wings spread, coiled on a dark grey stone plinth — came back as real surfaces: the wings, scales and plinth are geometry you can light, edit and print, and the guide names the invented sides (dorsal neck and spine, rear wing surfaces, the base's underside) as inventions. Flaws and limits: fine detail lives in the texture maps, not the geometry — 30,000 triangles is a budget, so whiskers, horns and wing membranes are approximate; the mesh arrives unrigged (the model offers rigging as an option, left off here — a rigid statue may not rig, and a failed rig fails the generation); one object per run; scale is relative, not measured. The probe GLB parsed at exactly 30,000 triangles; the saved run's parses at 29,992. Unlike the splat, the mesh GLB is Y-up and renders in the stock viewer — no points path needed. The turntable is a plain flat-shaded render of the saved GLB with its own base-colour texture, not a beauty shot — orbit the workflow's sampled GLB in the editor for the real thing.

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Swap Object photo for your own photo of one object — whole, in frame, on an uncluttered background works best. Keep Rebuild as a mesh on speridlabs/idol/max/turbo with PBR on; for a character or creature, the model's rigging option adds a skeleton and animation clips at $0.125 (rigid props may not rig, and a failed rig fails the run). Idol Max ($0.20) is the stereo-depth quality variant of the same call.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. Three model calls: the saved run reported $0.1029 in total (Idol Max Turbo a flat $0.10 with PBR included, Gemini survey $0.0029, GLM guide $0.00). Prices and results vary.
  4. Orbit the saved GLB in the workflow and compare it with the photo: surfaces and textures this time, not points — then read the guide's honest edges before you build on it. Fine detail lives in the 2048px texture maps, not the geometry; 30,000 triangles is a budget. The saved run's GLB parses at 29,992 triangles with UVs and three embedded maps.
