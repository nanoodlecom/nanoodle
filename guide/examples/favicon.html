What this workflow is for

Turn a short brand brief into a raster favicon concept you can inspect at the sizes an app actually uses.

How to use it

  1. Open the graph with the button below. Save current work first — loading an example replaces the canvas (Undo restores it).
  2. Edit the inputs. Change Brand — name, symbol, and colors. The LLM condenses that brief; Muse renders one square image.
  3. Inspect the result. Look at the result at 16 pixels. You want one simple glyph, the requested palette, and a readable silhouette. This is a raster concept: export or convert to the icon formats your app needs as a separate step. No letters in the sample brief.
  4. Typical cost. The reviewed run reported $0.01. The image step is listed at $0.01, plus a small text call. Prices and results vary.
