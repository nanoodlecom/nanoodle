Event details to a bilingual poster

Turn three lines of event details into a poster lettered in Chinese and English.

Every Chinese and English line exact on the first run · 2026-10-19 · Reported saved run: $0.07879. z-ai/glm-5.3-flash · moss-image

Show what MOSS Image does best: exact text in two scripts, placed where the brief says. The LLM writes every line to print in quotes, with its position; MOSS letters them.

Saved result

GLM 5.3 Flash turns three lines of event details into a poster brief with every line to print in quotes: 秋茶会, Autumn Tea Festival, 10月24日–26日 10:00–18:00 / Oct 24–26, 10am–6pm, and 免费品茗 / Free Tastings. MOSS Image letters all of it at 1024×1536 with a negative prompt, character for character and where the brief puts it, with no extra words, over a line-art teapot and cup on textured paper. Flaws: the illustration is pale next to the lettering, the brief chose 秋茶会 rather than a longer headline, and MOSS is not fast (this run took about half a minute; test images took 19 to 78 seconds against 13 to 19 for Nano Banana 2.1). In side-by-side tests MOSS kept a two-column bilingual menu layout that Nano Banana 2.1, FLUX.3 and GPT Image 2 each rearranged. The poster was checked and is SFW (no people).

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Rewrite Event details and ▶ Run. Bilingual poster brief holds the copywriting rules (headline length, which lines, no people or logos). The MOSS node carries a Negative prompt; Steps, Guidance and Prompt expansion are under ⚙️ Model options and stay at the model defaults when blank.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. One GLM 5.3 Flash call for $0.0002 and one MOSS Image call at 1024×1536 for $0.079. The saved run was $0.079. At MOSS's default 1024×1024 it is about $0.053, and at 512×512 about $0.013.
  4. Read every printed line against the brief, character by character, in both scripts. Look for extra words, a dropped line, or text in the wrong place.
