References that went in

Upload one product photo and write three edits — a backdrop, a set of props, the offer text — and Seedream 5.5 (bytedance/seedream-v5.5/edit) makes them one at a time, each round editing the previous round's result. Before the chain starts, a Gemini reader writes the product anchors, transcribing every word on the label; after it, a second Gemini re-reads the finished poster blind; a GLM drift report holds anchors, requested edits and re-read against each other and prints HELD, DRIFTED and CAMPAIGN: COMPLETE or INCOMPLETE. Why this model: Seedream 5.5 is ByteDance's November 2026 editor, sold on multi-round localized editing that leaves the rest of the picture alone — the exact claim a seller building a 12.12 campaign kit from one product shot is betting on — and ComfyUI took it as a Partner Node on Nov 11 while NanoGPT has carried it since Nov 6 at $0.028 per 1K edit. In the saved run the candle's label — EMBER & OAK / No. 12 / CEDAR + SMOKE / HAND POURED · 8 OZ — survives all three rounds word-for-word, the cedar discs and eucalyptus land beside it, and the poster text reads exactly as written. Flaws and limits, honestly: the drift report's two DRIFTED notes are about the readers' wording, not the pixels (one read called the lid 'open', the other did not; the re-read called the wall 'soft-focus' though no blur was requested) — the audit grades from text, so it flags wording wobble as readily as pixel drift. At pixel level the headline picked up a decorative opening quote mark the instruction never asked for; the words themselves are exact. The edit nodes' size is pinned to 1k — Seedream takes resolution tokens, not pixel sizes — and the model id is the /edit variant, the image-to-image route. A first full attempt billed $0.0591 before a service restart killed the runner and its outputs with it; the saved run is the re-run, and the probe that proved the endpoint first cost $0.028. The demo candle was painted by the media pipeline because the flow takes any upload — swap in a real product photo and it runs as saved.

  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. Six model calls: the saved run reported $0.0905 in total (three Seedream 5.5 edits at $0.028 each, Gemini anchors $0.0028, blind re-read $0.0038, GLM drift report $0.00). Prices and results vary.
  4. Read the poster's label against your original photo word by word, then read the Drift report. HELD and DRIFTED are graded from the blind re-read's text, not the pixels — in the saved run the report flagged two wording wobbles between the readers while the label itself held word-for-word, and the headline picked up a decorative quote mark in the pixels that neither reader transcribed. The audit is a tripwire, not a proof.
