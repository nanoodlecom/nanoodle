A creator ad for your product

Turn a product and a two-line brief into a vertical, creator-style talking ad.

Every sampled frame keeps the same face and the same pot; a Whisper pass on the ad's audio matched the script word for word · 2026-10-30 · Reported saved run: $0.614. meta/muse-image/text-to-image · meta/muse-image/edit · z-ai/glm-5.3-flash · Minimax-Speech-2.8-HD · longcat-avatar-1.5

Show how to chain a reference mix into a talking avatar. Muse Edit makes one frame with the creator holding the product, an LLM turns the brief into a short spoken script, and LongCat Avatar animates that frame to the voice, so the product stays in her hand for the whole ad.

Saved result

Muse paints a stand-in product: a terracotta self-watering herb pot with basil, sitting in a sage-green reservoir with a round water-level window. It also paints an invented creator in a mustard sweater filming a selfie in a sunny kitchen. Muse Edit puts that exact pot in her hand at chest height in a 9:16 frame, keeping her face, the kitchen and the pot's reservoir and window. GLM writes a 37-word first-person script from a two-line product brief: "I used to kill every basil I brought home. This little terracotta pot sits in a water reservoir, so it drinks on its own, and a tiny window shows me when to refill—like every two weeks. Honestly, get one." MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD reads it in 13 seconds, and LongCat Avatar 1.5 makes her say it at 480p while she holds the pot steady beside her shoulder. Flaws: the prompt asked for shoulder-length hair and she has longer curls. Her expressions run a little big, with a few wide-mouthed frames. The ad is 480p. The script ran 13.1 s against a 10 to 12 s target, so LongCat billed 14 s ($0.56). The editor's estimate for this node follows the voice length (script words before a run, the measured clip after). The creator is an invented person and the ad is AI-generated, so label it that way if you post it. Swap the product Image for a 📷 Image input to use your own product photo. Every still and sampled video frame was checked and is SFW (a clothed invented adult and a plant pot, no text or logos). Inspired by @teddarling's AI Creator Lab for ComfyUI (https://x.com/teddarling/status/2116187911850701017). No media from that post is used.

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Rewrite Product · stand-in for your product, or swap the Product shot Image for a 📷 Image input with your own product photo. Put what it really does in Product brief, because the script only claims what the brief says. Change Creator · look for a different invented creator or setting, keep "face and mouth fully visible" in Hold the product · instruction so the lip sync has a clear mouth, and set the tone in Delivery. Pick another voice on the Voice node.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key.   4. Check that the selfie frame keeps your product's shape and details and doesn't cover her mouth, read the script for claims the brief doesn't make, and watch the ad with sound to judge the lip sync and whether the product stays steady.
