References that went in

Upload a product photo, name a scene, and the product is moved without being repainted. Gemini first writes a preservation spec of the original: subject, colours, silhouette, and every printed word transcribed line by line. BRIA RMBG-3.0 — new on NanoGPT today, a matting model built to hold product text, logos and fine hair — cuts the subject out on transparency, so its pixels are lifted rather than redrawn. Nano Banana Edit then builds the new scene around the untouched cut-out under an instruction not to alter it. A second Gemini, blind to the original, re-reads the placed subject, and a GLM checker holds the two reads against each other and rules on the label and the verdict. In the saved run the bag travels from a kitchen counter to a golden-hour alpine cafe terrace and the placement is beautiful: headline label NORTH PEAK / DARK ROAST / 250g letter-perfect at full resolution. The checker still ruled NEEDS WORK — the blind re-read's condition line flagged the bottom microprint ('ROASTED IN THE MOUNTAINS • 100% ARABICA') as warped, and at full size that line is indeed the softest on the bag. That catch is the point of the flow: placement tools usually ask whether a composite looks good; this one asks whether it is still the same product, and prints the answer. Flaws and limits: the placement is a generative edit, so 'untouched' is an instruction the audit verifies, not a pixel guarantee — a true alpha-composite step would make it exact. The audit compares two vision transcriptions, so microprint near reader resolution can be flagged on a soft-but-faithful rendering, as arguably happened here. The cut-out keeps the subject's original lighting; the editor harmonises the scene around it rather than relighting the subject. One subject per run. The demo photo was itself painted by Muse Image in a separate $0.011 run because the flow takes any upload — swap in a real product photo and it runs as saved.

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Swap Product photo for your own product shot and rewrite New scene to wherever it should go. Leave Placement brief and the checker's system prompt in place — they carry the do-not-alter instruction and the verdict shape. Keep Cut out on bria/rmbg-3.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. Five model calls: the saved run reported $0.0906 in total (spec $0.0048, RMBG-3.0 cut-out $0.018, Nano Banana placement $0.039, blind re-read $0.0288, GLM checker $0.00). Prices and results vary.
  4. Compare the original, the cut-out and the placed composite, then read the Preservation verdict. PRESERVED means every spec line was re-read exactly; DRIFTED quotes the lines that differ. In the saved run the headline label was letter-perfect and the checker still flagged the microprint line — that strictness is the feature, not a failure of the demo.
