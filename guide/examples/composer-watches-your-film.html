A composer that watches your film

Score a clip with music written for what happens on screen, not a generic mood.

The fitted score enters on the first spark and is still playing when the lanterns rise; the blind one fades out early · 2026-10-30 · Reported saved run: $0.468. minimax/h3-max-turbo · elorian/glimpse · elevenlabs/music

Show video → LLM → music. Frames turns the clip into stills, a vision LLM writes a timed brief from them, and the music node composes from that brief. The blind branch beside it is the control.

Saved result

MiniMax H3 Max Turbo films 10 silent seconds of a lantern on a still mountain lake at night: a firefly drifts down, the lantern lights, and a wave of lanterns lights and rises across the water. Frames pulls one still per second, and Elorian Glimpse reads them and writes a composer's brief with tempo, key, instruments and cue points ("at 4 s, when the lantern ignites, strings swell"). ElevenLabs Music composes from that brief and Soundtrack lays it on the film. A second branch scores the same film from a text description only, so you can hear what watching adds. All four of Glimpse's cue points land within about half a second of the picture, and the fitted cue tracks the asked 60 BPM. Flaws: ElevenLabs ignored the 10 s duration on the fitted call and returned 19 s, so the fitted version stops at 10 s while still at full level. The hush asked for at 3 s is only a small dip. Glimpse sees 1 fps stills, so it called the firefly a spark. The clips were re-encoded for the gallery. Every sampled frame was checked and is SFW (lanterns on a lake, no people).

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Change Shot for a different film, or swap the film node for a 📹 Video input to score your own clip. Change Ask to ask for a different style, or set the number of frames to match the clip length. Edit Blind brief (no frames) to match, or delete the blind branch once you have seen the difference.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. H3 Max Turbo 10 s at 480p $0.25 + Glimpse $0.018 + two ElevenLabs Music cues at $0.10 each. Scoring your own clip costs about $0.12. The saved run was $0.468.
  4. Read the brief against the frames, then play both versions and listen at each cue point. Check the music's length, since music models can ignore the requested duration.
