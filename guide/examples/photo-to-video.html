Animate a still

Generate a first frame, then animate its steam.

Frame review · 2026-10-23 · Reported saved run: $0.261. meta/muse-image/text-to-image · minimax-h3-singularity/image-to-video

Muse draws a mug of tea beside a notebook. MiniMax H3 uses that first frame and a separate motion brief to make a five-second clip. Keeping the scene and movement in separate inputs lets you change what moves without redesigning the still.

Saved result

All 124 frames of the 23 October 2026 clip were checked and are SFW. It is 832×480 and 5.17 seconds, from minimax-h3-singularity/image-to-video at 480p after the Muse still. Later frames differ from the first by only a few levels out of 255, so the mug, desk and notebook stay in place. Watch the clip to judge the steam; a seamless loop is not guaranteed.

Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Change Still brief and Motion brief to describe the same scene. Muse generates the first frame; MiniMax H3 animates it for five seconds at 480p.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. The saved tea-mug run reported $0.261 on 23 October 2026: the Muse still plus five seconds of MiniMax H3 image-to-video at 480p. Prices and results vary.
  4. Play the clip. The mug, notebook and desk should stay stable while the steam moves. Check the full clip before using it.
