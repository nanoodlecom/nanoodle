Anime art to a cosplay photo

Turn an anime character drawing into a realistic cosplay photo of an invented adult cosplayer.

The photo keeps the hair colour, jacket stripes and lining, trousers, sneakers, umbrella and pocket watch; Gemini misread one braid as two · 2026-11-02 · Reported saved run: $0.02432. meta/muse-image/text-to-image · google/gemini-3.8-flash

Show how a vision LLM can sit between two image models. Gemini looks at the drawing and writes its features down as text, and the photo model renders only that text, so you can read, fix or reuse the feature list before paying for the photo.

Saved result

Muse paints an original anime character: a young adult woman with long straight teal hair, blunt bangs and a braid tied with a brass ring, amber eyes, a cropped cobalt-blue bomber jacket with white sleeve stripes and an orange quilted lining, a black high-neck top, charcoal cargo trousers with knee pads, white high-tops, a closed clear umbrella with an orange handle on her shoulder and a brass pocket watch at her hip, on a rainy rooftop at dusk. Gemini 3.8 Flash looks at that picture and writes a feature list (hair, eyes, face, outfit, props, pose, background) and a photo prompt for an invented adult cosplayer wearing the outfit as a real costume. Muse renders the photo from that text alone; the drawing itself never reaches the photo model, so everything carried over went through the written features. The photo keeps the teal wig and bangs, the cobalt jacket with white stripes and orange quilted lining, the black top, the knee-pad cargo trousers, the white high-tops, the pocket watch on a chain, the clear umbrella with orange ends on her shoulder, and the wet rooftop and city lights at dusk. Flaws: the drawing shows one braid, but Gemini read two, so the photo has two. The wig is longer than the drawn hair, down to her knees. The eyes come out a glowing orange-red instead of amber. The umbrella grows orange tips on its ribs and an orange strap. The jacket hangs a little lower than the cropped drawing. Gemini also picked an ethnicity for the cosplayer that the drawing doesn't state; edit its system prompt if you want to choose that yourself. Swap the Anime art Image for a 📷 Image input to read your own original drawing; use characters you made, not real people or existing IP. Both stills were checked and are SFW (a fully clothed invented adult, no text or logos). Inspired by @SuskyH99882's Anime → Realistic Cosplay ComfyUI workflow (https://x.com/SuskyH99882/status/2116568710563717316), where a vision LLM extracts the features and rebuilds them as a photo. Make it yours

  1. Open the graph — save first if the canvas already has your work. Undo brings yours back.
  2. Rewrite Anime character · invent for a different original character, or swap the Anime art Image for a 📷 Image input with your own drawing (characters you made, not real people or existing IP). Edit the system prompt on Read the features · Gemini to change the cosplayer, the location or the photo style, and change the size on Cosplay photo for a different frame.
  3. Check the editor estimate, then run with your NanoGPT key. Muse 2:3 $0.011 + Gemini 3.8 Flash about $0.002 + Muse 2:3 $0.011. The saved run was $0.024.
  4. Read the feature list against the drawing first, since anything Gemini misreads (here, one braid became two) carries into the photo. Then compare hair, outfit colours, stripes, props and pose between the drawing and the photo.
