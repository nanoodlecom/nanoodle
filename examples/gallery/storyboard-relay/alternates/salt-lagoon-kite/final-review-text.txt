### Story Beat
Frame 1 establishes the crab on the wooden pier holding the spool, looking toward the distant sandbar with the kite resting nearby. In Frame 2, the action successfully advances: the crab relocates to the pink sandbar, holds down its straw hat against the wind with one claw, and flies the yellow diamond kite with the other via a taut white string. The new wind-blown action reads clearly.
