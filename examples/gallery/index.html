Muse paints a color anime-style panel: a cartoon fox in a yellow raincoat carries a red paper lantern down a rainy old-town street at night, with a cat asleep on a windowsill, laundry overhead and a small bridge behind. Qwen Image 2.1 Edit redraws it in one pass as a black-and-white manga panel. The composition, pose and every background element stay in place. Outlines come back as crisp ink, the night sky and shadows become solid blacks and dense dots, walls and cobblestones get screentone, and the lantern glow stays white paper.
