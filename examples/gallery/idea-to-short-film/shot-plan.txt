STYLE BIBLE (pasted word for word before every image and shot prompt)

A small yellow paper boat, about eight inches long, folded from glossy bright-yellow paper with crisp visible creases, is the hero. A child appears only as a small pale hand in a red raincoat sleeve, wrist and fingers only. The setting is a city sidewalk gutter with fast-moving rainwater, concrete curbs, dark wet asphalt. Overcast rainy late afternoon, steady grey drizzle throughout. Soft diffused grey daylight, muted reflections in the water. Restrained palette: yellow, red, grey, wet black. Shot on a 35mm lens at child's eye level, low camera height near the gutter. Naturalistic live-action film look, shallow depth of field, subtle grain, gentle overcast color grade.

STORY

SHOT 1 — SETUP
START: Rainy afternoon, a curb-side gutter streaming with rainwater; a small hand holds a yellow paper boat above the current.
ACTION: The hand lowers the boat onto the water and releases it; camera tracks alongside as the current carries it forward.
END: The boat glides down the gutter, rain rippling the water around it.

SHOT 2 — TURN
START: The boat gliding down the gutter, rain rippling the water around it.
ACTION: The current quickens and the boat is pulled toward the open storm drain, spinning on its edge; camera pushes in close on the tilting boat.
END: The boat balanced at the drain's lip, half tilted over the dark opening, water pouring past.

SHOT 3 — PAYOFF
START: The boat balanced at the drain's lip, half tilted over the dark opening, water pouring past.
ACTION: The small hand reaches in from above and plucks the boat out of the water; camera pulls back as the hand lifts it clear, safe against the gray rain.
END: The hand holding the dripping yellow boat aloft above the empty drain, rain falling steadily.
