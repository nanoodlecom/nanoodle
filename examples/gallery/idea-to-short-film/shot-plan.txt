HERO: A tiny hand-folded bright yellow paper boat, small as a child's palm, crisp folded creases, slightly dampened at the bow.

STYLE: 35mm film look with soft grain, deep night blues and purples cut by neon pinks and oranges warming to golden sunrise, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares, wet reflective surfaces.

SHOT 1: Extreme close-up at gutter level, a child's hand lowers the yellow paper boat into a rushing rain-filled gutter beside a curb; camera slowly pushes in as fingers release and the current catches the boat, neon signs reflecting in the dark water around it.

SHOT 2: Low tracking shot gliding alongside the boat at water level as it races down the gutter channel, camera moving parallel in a steady lateral dolly; rain streaks fall and the boat bobs and tilts through splashes, weaving past glowing pink and cyan reflections stretching across the wet asphalt.

SHOT 3: Wide shot from behind and slightly above as the gutter empties into a calm harbor; camera cranes up slowly as the yellow paper boat drifts out onto still water, the rising sun painting the sky gold, its small silhouette growing peaceful against the vast glowing horizon.
