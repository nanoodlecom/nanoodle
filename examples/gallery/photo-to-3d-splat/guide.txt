WHAT YOU GOT — this is a Gaussian splat of a low-top casual sneaker: hundreds of thousands of soft coloured points that together read as a solid object you can orbit, rebuilt from a single lateral, slightly elevated photo. It ships here as a GLB point-cloud preview. It is a point splat, not a polygon mesh — there are no surfaces, UVs or textures to edit. Idol also returns a full PLY splat on the raw API, but that is API-only; what you have in hand is the GLB preview.

HOW TO OPEN IT — the GLB preview opens in any GLB viewer or Blender. If you fetch the PLY from the API, open it in a splat viewer such as SuperSplat, PlayCanvas, or most Gaussian-splat tools.

THE HONEST EDGES — every side the photo did not show is invented: the symmetrical medial side, the treaded gum outsole bottom, the heel rear, and the shoe interior. Thin or soft parts are approximate, especially the fabric laces, eyelets, canvas weave, and suede texture. The splat holds one object only, and its scale is relative, not measured.
