WHAT YOU GOT
This is a Gaussian splat of a winged dragon figurine on its rectangular plinth: hundreds of thousands of soft coloured points that together read as a solid, orbitable object — antiqued bronze with dark patina over a dark grey stone base. It is delivered as a GLB point-cloud preview. To be plain: this is a point splat, not a polygon mesh. There are no surfaces, no UVs and no textures to edit. Idol also returns a full PLY splat on the raw API, but that is API-only; what you have in hand here is the GLB preview.

HOW TO OPEN IT
The GLB preview opens in any GLB viewer, or in Blender if you prefer working there. If you fetch the PLY from the API instead, open it in a splat viewer such as SuperSplat, PlayCanvas or most Gaussian-splat tools.

THE HONEST EDGES
Idol invented every side the photo did not show: the posterior side of the wings, the rear spine, the back of the body and the plinth underside are guesses, not observations. Thin wing membranes, delicate facial whiskers, sharp horns and spiky spinal crests are approximate at best. The splat holds one object only, and its scale is relative, not measured.
