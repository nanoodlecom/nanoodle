=== blockout-writer ===
Grayscale Blender blockout, 16:9 cinematic still. One boxy courier robot with stubby wheel-leg blocks mid-leap across a narrow gap between two untextured gray rooftop buildings, body tilted forward, limbs tucked. Low tracking camera on the near rooftop at wheel height, framing the robot against the far roof where it will land. No second robot, no colors, no textures. Flat soft gray studio light, simple matte geometry.

=== look-writer ===
Give the courier robot a matte off-white shell with scuffed orange accent panels and a dark rubberized wheel-leg tread, its chassis rimmed with dust. The brick buildings are weathered red-brown with mortar stains, fire escapes, and pigeons scattering off the far ledge. Dusk light: deep indigo sky fading to burnt amber at the horizon, warm rim light on the robot's edges, cool blue shadow fill in the alley. Lens feel: anamorphic 35mm, slight haze, subtle lens flare from the low sun, motion blur on the wheels.

=== motion-writer ===
The robot compresses on the ledge and pushes off, wheels tucking as it arcs across the alley, then lands on the far rooftop with a soft crouch that settles into stillness. The camera tracks low alongside the jump, drifting forward to keep the robot centered, easing to a stop as it holds the crouch on the far roof. No new characters enter the frame.
