WHAT YOU MADE — This video is a still photograph of an ancient marble emperor, used as the photo's subject, performing one spoken line. The mouth movement is driven entirely by the voice clip, not generated speech. The line performed is: "Two thousand years silent... and you wake me NOW?" The emperor appears to speak these words directly, as if woken after two thousand years and not pleased about it.

HOW IT WORKS — The script line was performed by a Gemini TTS voice, Algenib, delivering the old man's gravelly, clipped reading. The audio was trimmed to five seconds. LTX-2.5 Fast then took the photo and that audio as direct inputs, with generated audio turned off, so the clip carries only the original voice track. The clip's length follows the audio's length. Note that audio-driven runs render at 1080p even when 720p is selected.

THE HONEST EDGES — Billing is per second: $0.14 a second at the audio-driven 1080p rate, so this five-second clip is about $0.70, plus a few cents for the speech. Keep lines to about ten words. No current model reliably closes the mouth on pauses; in a frame-counted shootout, LTX was best of the field and still closed the mouth on only 2 of 6 pauses. Short lines with few pauses forgive most. Front-facing faces with an unobstructed mouth work best, and statues and paintings forgive more than photographs of real people — which suits a marble emperor well.
